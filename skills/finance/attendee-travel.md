# Attendee travel

1. Read the current event travel policy, attendee approval/award, itinerary and
   expense documents available in the scoped case. Determine who owes whom,
   eligible category, supported amount/currency and any explicit cap. No policy
   supplied means the cap and eligibility are unknown, not unlimited.
2. Distinguish a stipend award, reimbursement for an actual paid expense, unpaid
   booking/vendor charge and sponsor-funded travel. Check existing obligations
   and settlement evidence to avoid duplicate reimbursements or a second payment
   for the same flight/hotel cost.
3. Use current budget and kernel forecast/simulation to explain financial impact.
   Create/update a source-linked expected/reported payable only where the actual
   documents and director's policy support it. Keep travel separate from general
   organizer reimbursements and prize obligations.
4. Missing payer identity, ticket/receipt linkage, attendee eligibility or an
   operative cap needs a focused question. Exact verified payment destination,
   testnet asset/network and amount are required before approval preparation;
   the service separately enforces the event's human approval policy.
5. There is no flight/hotel booking, ticket cancellation, passport handling,
   immigration advice, currency conversion, tax filing or bank payout tool.
   Explain those limits, record the necessary human follow-up, and never describe
   a proposed stipend as delivered or a reported payment as confirmed settlement.
