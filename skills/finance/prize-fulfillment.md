# Prize fulfillment

1. Establish the award, winning recipient/team, amount/currency and actual payer
   from the director's approved award record and sponsor agreement. Do not assume
   every sponsor prize is paid by the event.
2. Read existing obligations and sources to avoid a second prize payable. Ask
   the director about disputed winner identity, prize splits, restrictions or
   missing policy. A model cannot invent an approved award or recipient consent.
3. A documented event-funded cash prize may be recorded as a source-linked
   expected/reported payable through `propose_obligation_update`. Preserve the
   prize category in the title/finding; do not relabel it an organizer expense.
4. Prepare an exact approval action only after recipient destination, payable,
   testnet asset/network and amount are verified. Current event human approvals
   remain enforced by the service. The actor neither signs nor sends funds.
5. There are no merchandise shipping, gift-card purchase, tax-document collection,
   withholding, tax filing or prize-platform integrations. Record what is missing
   and route those decisions to the director/appropriate authority. Do not infer
   legal tax treatment from another event's anecdote.
