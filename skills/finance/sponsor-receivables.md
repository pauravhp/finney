# Sponsor receivables

1. Read the latest signed agreement or clearly labeled pledge, amendments,
   sponsor correspondence and existing receivables. Preserve source versions.
   Separate a prospect or tentative offer from a documented commitment.
2. Identify amount/currency, due date, responsible payer and deliverables that
   affect collection. Flag conflicting amendments or unclear legal terms for the
   director; the actor cannot give a binding legal interpretation or sign terms.
3. Use `propose_obligation_update` with direction `receivable` to record a
   documented expected/reported sponsor amount, or update its existing version.
   Check for duplicates first. Sponsor income does not use a vendor-payment
   approval action, and an expected receivable does not increase confirmed cash.
4. Inspect current financial position and records before saying payment arrived.
   An email saying "sent" is reported evidence. Only the separate trusted income
   import/reconciliation path may post cash; this actor has no income-posting or
   bank-access tool and cannot mark a receivable settled from its own text.
5. Record an accurate status and, if useful, draft a source-specific collection
   question in the case finding. No mail is sent. Distinguish sponsor award
   responsibilities from event-funded prizes unless an actual agreement makes
   the event the payer.
