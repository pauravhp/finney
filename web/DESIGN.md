# Finney Organizer’s Desk

The owner-approved v7 design is the visual authority: Albert Sans, ink and mint,
a receipt character, layered colored folders on the landing, and a pale green
sidebar with white working surfaces. This replaces the earlier dark lilac build.

The landing is a Persuade surface, transferred from the approved original artwork.
Its folder interaction and scroll-driven Harness² explanation remain intact.
Financial examples and the learning animation are labeled as illustrations. Every
operating CTA opens `/desk`. The menu-bar teaser remains a labeled concept; it does
not imply a shipped native application.

The desk is an Operate surface. Its controls bind to the existing authenticated,
scoped application APIs and database snapshots. Approval, payment, procedure,
source, report and connection controls retain their real behavior. The reference
concept's scripted payments, timer-driven agent output and invented balances are
not part of the operating app.

The receipt mark and wordmark identify the product. A compact Ask Finney action
is available in the top bar. The workbench supports a general or case-related
prompt, manual record, or document upload. Expanded cases expose an additional
upload surface for more evidence or an explicitly identified document revision.
All such forms preserve scope, source identity and server-side validation.

Money uses tabular figures. Unknown values remain unknown. Status color always
has text. Approvals remain associated with the exact action hash and current
policy. Learning views show actual stored results, including rejected candidates.

On mobile the navigation becomes a drawer, fields stack, controls remain at least
44px where practical, and long source names and records wrap. Tables scroll inside
their own frame. Reduced-motion preferences suppress character animation and use
the landing’s static explanation. Keyboard focus stays visible.

The final desk adopts v7’s composition as well as its palette. Work is the initial
view, with the prompt/upload composer beside an On file rail. Navigation follows
Work, To review, Overview, Reports and Agent; Activity and Connections retain
necessary real operational tools. A case opens into source-document tabs and an
activity pane beside Finney’s recorded finding, stage blockers, exact-action
approval/payment controls and director conversation. The decision moves above
evidence on phones. The initial palette-only overview arrangement was superseded
before release. General prompt drafts survive tab changes within the event scope.

## October 25: director scenario redesign

The owner superseded the blank workbench-first v7 composition after reviewing live screenshots. The receipt mark, Albert Sans and mint/ink identity stay; the first screen is now a concrete mid-event situation with one action. A resumable, optional walkthrough invokes actual scoped work and opens actual decisions. It never marks model work complete from tutorial progress.

Case evidence renders the complete nested source body. Case-specific upload and conversation are bound to that case; their generic task tabs/case selector are removed. Long reasoning, raw identifiers and system provenance move into disclosures. Current amounts, stage requirements, refusals and unknowns stay explicit. Sources retain exact org/run/version/path binding and predecessor history. Body copy is 16px with a 14px support-text floor. Motion is limited to a short step transition and disabled under reduced-motion.

The page’s first question is “What needs my decision at this event?” Its signature interaction is the changed invoice alongside the stored review, followed by a real director decision and recovery. See `../docs/build/UI_TEXT_AUDIT.md` for the complete surface audit and verification limits.
